## 👤 About Me

Born in Portugal, living in Switzerland since I was 12. I hold a Swiss **ES diploma in IT** (*Informaticien dipl. ES*) from **CPNV** *(Centre Professionnel du Nord Vaudois)*, specializing in infrastructure.

Whether it's spinning up a homelab, provisioning infrastructure as code, or automating something that used to be manual, I thrive on understanding *how* things work well enough to rebuild them better.
